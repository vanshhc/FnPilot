# Community conduct

Treat people with respect. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and abilities. Discuss the work and support claims with evidence. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose private information, or retaliate against a report.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Explain moderation decisions where privacy allows. Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through their GitHub profile; do not publish another person's private information in an issue. Maintainers should recuse themselves from decisions involving their own conduct.
